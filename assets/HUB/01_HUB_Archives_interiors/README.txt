DRIFTER — Individual Asset Pack
================================

Each prop / shell piece is supplied as its own transparent RGBA PNG.
Sprite crops preserve the source sheet pixel scale; 24 px of transparent padding is retained around each piece.
No sprites were rescaled.

  Maintenance_Depot/Props/  — 6 individual props

shell, props, and combined reference atlases, plus README.txt and manifest.json.

  Watchtower/          — 4 shell pieces, 6 props
  Checkpoint_Booth/    — 4 shell pieces, 6 props

Individual sprites are supplied as transparent PNGs. Atlas and asset paths in
each location manifest are relative to that location folder.
